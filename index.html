    How the twelve weeks run

    1
    Weeks 1–3 · Pressure-test the idea

    Your mentor takes the idea apart with you — who exactly has this problem, what they do about it today, and whether they'd pay. You talk to real people in your market instead of guessing from your desk.

    A problem statement you can defend
    2
    Weeks 4–8 · Build the smallest real thing

    Not the full product — the smallest version that proves the point. Weekly sessions on scope, what to cut, and what "done enough to show someone" actually means for your business.

    A first version in front of real users
    3
    Weeks 9–12 · Learn, then decide

    What the first users actually did, not what they politely said. Your mentor helps you read the signal honestly and choose: double down, change direction, or stop — which is sometimes the right answer.

    A decision you can back with evidence

    You walk away with

    • A written, evidenced problem statement
    • Notes from real conversations with your market
    • A shipped first version, however small
    • A prioritised list of what to fix next
    • A go / change / stop call you can defend to anyone
    Not for you if… you already have paying customers and simply need more of them — that's Sales Support. Or if you want someone to build the product for you; your mentor works alongside you, not instead of you.
    Programme 02 · Sell

    Sales Support for Founders

    For founders who already have something people can buy. You finish knowing exactly where deals were dying — and with a weekly sales routine you can run without us.

    Duration12 weeks
    CadenceWeekly working session
    Between sessionsLive deal support
    On-groundJoint customer visits, where available

    How the twelve weeks run

    1
    Weeks 1–2 · Find where deals actually die

    We go through your real conversations from the last few months — who you approached, what you said, where they went quiet. Most founders are wrong about which step is broken, and it's cheaper to find out here.

    A funnel map with the real leak marked
    2
    Weeks 3–8 · Rebuild the broken part

    Whether that's the opening line, the pricing, the follow-up, or who you're approaching in the first place. You re-pitch live to real buyers between sessions and bring back what happened.

    A pitch and price tested on real buyers
    3
    Weeks 9–12 · Make it a routine

    Sales stops being something you do when you remember. We build the weekly rhythm — how many conversations, tracked where, followed up when — and run it together until it holds without your mentor.

    A weekly sales routine that runs without us

    You walk away with

    • A funnel map showing where you actually lose deals
    • A rewritten pitch, tested on real buyers
    • A pricing position you can hold without discounting
    • Outreach and follow-up you'll actually keep doing
    • A simple pipeline you can run yourself
    Not for you if… nobody can buy from you yet — start with Mentorship instead. Or if you're looking to hire a salesperson; this teaches you to sell, it isn't an outsourced sales team.

    Cadence, duration and phase content above are the proposed programme structure for this prototype — they are confirmed with you on the first call and adjusted to your stage, not applied identically to everyone.
